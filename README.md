# IT-Prompts-for-women-
IT and AI prompts to help women solve their tech issues
IT Support Prompts for Women 

## A free resource by Blessing Udoh — IT professional based in Manchester, UK.

This repo is for everyday women especially those in corporate who want to solve their own tech issues without always raising a ticket and waiting for IT support.

No jargon. No judgement. Just prompts that work.

-------------------------—--------------
### My laptop is running slowly
> "My laptop is running slowly. It's a Windows 11 device. 
What are the most common reasons and what can I check 
myself before calling IT support?"

### I can't connect to Wi-Fi
> "I can't connect to Wi-Fi on my laptop. I can see the network but it won't connect. Walk me through the steps to troubleshoot and fix this myself."

### I forgot my password
> "I've been locked out of my work account. What are the typical self-service steps I should try before logging a support ticket?"

### I don't understand what IT support told me
> "IT support told me [paste what they said]. Can you explain what this means in basic English and what I need to do?"

### How to write a good IT support ticket
> "Help me write a clear IT support ticket. My issue is 
[describe your problem]. I need it to include what the problem is, when it started, and what I've already tried so the Service Desk can fix this promptly."
